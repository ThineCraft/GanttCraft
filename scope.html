ThineCraft project scope

GanttCraft

Status: Placeholder project. This document describes intended direction; it does not claim that working functionality exists.

Project website

Purpose

GanttCraft is the planned open project scheduling and resource-management system for understandable plans, visible consequences, and durable project data.

Confirmed direction

  • Tasks, dependencies, milestones, constraints, baselines, and schedule history.
  • Clear visualization of how one change propagates through a plan.
  • Workload, availability, capacity, overload, slack, and competing commitments.
  • Connections to files, conversations, records, and workflows elsewhere in ThineCraft.
  • Exportable project records that remain independently usable.

Boundaries and non-goals

  • GanttCraft is not merely a chart renderer; schedule logic, resources, change, and history are central.
  • Plans should be legible to the people doing the work, not only planning specialists.
  • Files and conversations remain owned by their appropriate services and are linked into project context.
  • No implementation, import fidelity, scheduling engine, or production readiness is currently promised.

Expected integrations

  • ShareCraft for project sites, records, and governance.
  • DriveCraft for project files and offline continuity.
  • TeamCraft for discussions, meetings, and decisions.
  • FlowCraft for approvals, notifications, and automated project processes.
  • ShowCraft for explanatory and operational visualizations.

Open decisions

  • Scheduling engine, calendars, constraints, leveling, and critical-path behavior.
  • Resource identity, skills, cost, availability, and portfolio-level capacity.
  • Baseline, scenario, audit, and change-approval models.
  • Import, export, and interoperability targets for existing project-management formats.

Guidance for AI contributors

  1. Prioritize explainable schedules and visible consequences over decorative charts.
  2. Do not describe planned scheduling or resource capabilities as implemented.
  3. Keep project data portable and independently useful.
  4. Link external artifacts rather than silently duplicating their ownership.
  5. Record new product decisions in this file before presenting them as settled scope.
